FAQ

Is this a custom website?

It is a focused $999 website build with a 10-page structure and an AI web developer bench attached. The goal is to launch a strong small business site quickly, then give you a smarter way to keep improving it.

Does the agent replace a developer?

No. Think of it as your first web developer bench: it helps plan, write, structure, and maintain updates. For advanced custom code or complex integrations, a human can still step in.

Can I name the agent?

Yes. That is part of the product experience. Your Website Bench Agent should feel like a member of your SMB bench, not a generic tool.

What does "keep your site up to date" mean?

It means your Bench helps you create and organize updates: new offers, refreshed service copy, added FAQs, campaign pages, seasonal announcements, and content ideas. The exact publishing workflow depends on your site setup.

Who is this best for?

Small businesses that need a better website now, but also know the site will need regular changes as the business grows.
