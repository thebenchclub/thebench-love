About The Bench

Built by an operator who knows what overloaded work actually feels like.

The Bench was started by Christy Nolan in the Houston/Pearland area after nearly 25 years building products, operating systems, and enterprise workflows. The company exists for people carrying more than one person’s worth of work — founders, agents, small business owners, team parents, and households that need practical help, not another tool to manage.

Let's get startedEmail Christy

Christy’s background

Christy is a product-and-operations builder with experience across enterprise environments including Pfizer and NRG Energy. That background matters here: The Bench is not built around AI tricks. It is built around workflow clarity, handoffs, accountability, customer follow-up, and the everyday operational pressure that keeps good work stuck.

Why The Bench exists

Most people do not need a chatbot. They need a capable coworker that understands the job, remembers the context, and helps move the next task forward. The Bench packages AI automation as named coworkers — Benches — with roles, boundaries, and practical workflows.

What we build

  • Website and lead-capture systems for small businesses.
  • Realtor workflows for listings, follow-up, social, and pipeline support.
  • Household and family logistics support for invisible mental load.
  • Sports organization workflows for schedules, communication, and game analysis.

How we work

We build custom first, prove the workflow in the real world, then turn the working pattern into a repeatable Bench. The goal is not more software. The goal is to make the truth visible, make the next move clearer, and give overloaded people a usable system they can trust.
