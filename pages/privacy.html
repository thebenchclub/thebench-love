Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Bench Club collects, uses, and protects information when you visit thebench.love, request a service, download resources, or communicate with us.

Questions can be sent to christy@thebenchclub.ai.

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, business details, project details, service interests, and messages submitted through forms.

We may also collect basic technical and usage information, such as page views, referral source, device or browser type, IP-derived location, UTM parameters, and interactions with our pages or campaigns.

  • To protect our site, services, users, and business operations.

AI-Enabled Workflows

The Bench Club may use AI tools and automation systems to help organize requests, draft content, analyze project needs, build websites, route leads, and support client workflows. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the service you are requesting.

Cookies, Analytics, And Advertising

We may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, advertising platforms, and similar technologies to measure traffic, improve the site, track campaign performance, and support retargeting or lead generation campaigns.

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers and tools that help us operate the website, route leads, send communications, process forms, host services, analyze performance, or deliver requested work.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or opt-out assistance by emailing christy@thebenchclub.ai. You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in those emails where available.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, but no website, transmission, or storage system is completely secure.

Children

The Bench Club services are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last updated date.
